Phase 3

Content Strategy: Miguel Wilson Collection

Prepared: May 2026 Status: Confidential

Content Mission

Position Miguel Wilson Collection as Atlanta's definitive authority on elevated menswear, bespoke tailoring, and occasion dressing. Every post earns trust with the buyer who searches "custom suit Atlanta" or "best menswear designer Atlanta" and uses the brand's Instagram as a vetting surface before booking a consultation.

Content Pillars

Pillar 1: Behind the Fitting (40% of output)

The craft is the story. Fabric pulls from Italian and British mills, hand-pressing, lapel detailing, the consultation process at Phipps Plaza. This is the content that converts a curious follower into a consultation inquiry. It proves the work rather than describing it.

Format examples: - Reel: "This is what a proper sleeve pitch looks like" (measuring-room shot + finished garment comparison) - Carousel: "Three fabrics we're working with this season" (fabric swatches, names, mills, what occasion each fits) - Single image: Finished custom suit flat lay with brief copy on the client brief

Tone: Precise, proud, zero hype. Let the fabric and the cut carry the aspiration.

Pillar 2: Celebrity and Press Recaps (20% of output)

Cam Newton. 2 Chainz. Waka Flocka. Vogue. Modern Luxury Atlanta. Jezebel. These are earned credentials. The content strategy is not to post them once at launch and move on; it is to re-surface them with context, explaining the brief, the occasion, and the execution.

Format examples: - Single image: "[Name] at [occasion]. Custom [garment description]. Miguel Wilson Collection." Short caption, no hype. - Story: Press-mention screengrab with a brief "When Vogue calls, you answer" caption - Reel: "The brief behind the Cam Newton suit" (60-second voice-over if Miguel is willing to narrate)

Tone: Confident but not boastful. Let the credential carry the weight. Do not over-caption.

Pillar 3: Menswear Education (25% of output)

This is the pillar that drives organic discovery, saves, and shares. Men who do not yet know what "half-canvas construction" or "soft shoulder Neapolitan tailoring" means will learn it here and associate that knowledge with Miguel Wilson. When they are ready to buy, the brand that taught them is the brand they trust.

Format examples: - Carousel: "The difference between custom, made-to-measure, and off-the-rack" (3 slides, clear language) - Reel: "What makes a suit jacket fit correctly" (5 checkpoints in 45 seconds) - Single image: "Lapel anatomy" infographic with brief copy - Carousel: "How to build a 5-suit wardrobe from scratch" (occasion-mapped: boardroom, wedding, gala, Sunday best, casual luxury)

Tone: Educational without being condescending. Speak to the man who wants to know more, not the man who already does.

Pillar 4: Polo Classic Countdown and Event Coverage (15% of output)

The Annual Atlanta Fashion and Polo Classic is the brand's single biggest owned media moment. It deserves its own structured content arc: 6-week countdown, week-of coverage, event day, post-event recap, charity impact follow-up.

Format examples: - Countdown series: "6 weeks to the Polo Classic. This is what we're building." (tease a signature look per week) - Day-of Reel: event atmosphere, suits in motion, charity partner spotlight - Post-event carousel: best looks on the field, press coverage recap, "See you next year" CTA with email RSVP link

Tone: Celebratory, community-anchored, charity-adjacent. The Polo Classic is not just a fashion show; it is an Atlanta institution tied to the Olympics Foundation. The content should carry that weight.

Platform-Specific Notes

Instagram primary: All four pillars. Reels for education and behind-the-fitting. Carousels for education series. Single images for celebrity/press and event teasers.

Instagram Stories: Daily-ish. Fabric swatches, fitting room moments, event countdown tickers, consultation booking CTA slides, RSVP push for Polo Classic. Stories are the direct-response layer; feed posts are the authority layer.

Link-in-Bio: Single CTA rotating by season. During Polo Classic build: RSVP page. During wedding season (Q1 to Q2): wedding party consultation booking. During new collection drop: collection landing. Always one clear action, never a linktree of 8 options.

TikTok / YouTube Shorts (optional, Phase 2): Menswear education reels repurposed. "What makes a suit fit correctly" and "custom vs MTM vs off-the-rack" have high organic discovery potential on TikTok's algorithm.
