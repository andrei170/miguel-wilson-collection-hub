Prepared: May 2026 Status: Confidential

Funnel Overview

The Miguel Wilson Collection buyer arrives from one of four sources: Instagram (organic), press citation (Vogue/Modern Luxury reader), AI-search result (once schema is deployed), or direct referral. Each source has a different temperature and a different next step. This blueprint structures all four entry points into one cohesive funnel that ends at a booked consultation.

Conversion Economics

Funnel Stage Benchmark Conversion
Landing page visitor to lead magnet opt-in 15 to 25%
Lead magnet opt-in to email sequence open rate 40 to 55% (luxury niche)
Email sequence to consultation booking 5 to 12%
Consultation to closed sale 50 to 70% (bespoke, at this tier)
Wedding party: groom booking to groomsmen re-engagement (12 months) 25 to 35%
Polo Classic RSVP to post-event consultation 8 to 15%

Conservative 12-month model: 500 monthly hub visitors (from AEO + IG traffic post-schema deploy) x 20% opt-in rate = 100 new email subscribers/month x 8% consultation conversion = 8 consultations/month x 60% close rate = 4.8 new suits/month x $2,800 average = $13,440/month = $161,000/year

Plus: 2 wedding parties/month x $7,200 avg party = $172,800/year Plus: Polo Classic list of 500 captures x 10% post-event conversion x $2,800 = $140,000/year (once, recurring annually with new captures)

Combined first-year conservative: $473,000 in new traceable revenue from the funnel infrastructure.

Notes on Pricing Transparency

Per copy quality rules: all prices must be visible on landing page or labeled "consultation pricing" clearly, not "DM for details."

Recommended approach for this brand: - Custom suits: "Starting from $[X]. Contact us for your consultation." - Wedding parties: "Group rates available. Book a complimentary group consultation." - Lifestyle collection: Prices as labeled if e-commerce, or "View the collection" with clear pricing on product pages.

If Miguel does not want starting prices published, the minimum acceptable label is "Consultation pricing. Schedule a complimentary fitting to discuss your project." This is clear, not evasive.
