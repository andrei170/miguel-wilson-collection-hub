Email 2: The Custom Suit Process Explained

Subject: What actually happens at a custom suit consultation Preview: Sixty minutes. Twelve measurements. Six weeks to completion.

A lot of men have never been to a bespoke consultation. They assume it is complicated, expensive without explanation, or takes a year.

Here is what actually happens at Phipps Plaza.

You arrive for sixty minutes. We take twelve measurements. We walk through the fabric room together: weight, weave, country of mill, occasion. You tell me where the suit is going. I tell you what construction serves it best. We agree on the details: lapel, button stance, lining, finish.

Six to eight weeks later, you return for a first fitting. The suit is 85% complete. We pin, mark, and note. Two weeks after that: the final fitting. By pickup day, the suit has been hand-pressed and is ready.

That is bespoke. No templates. No size chart. A suit built around your body and your occasion.

Email 3: The Polo Classic Story

Subject: Why we run the Polo Classic every year Preview: Five years in. Still the best Saturday in Atlanta.

Five years ago, the Atlanta Fashion and Polo Classic was an idea. Atlanta needed a luxury fashion event that was also a community event. One that dressed the city and gave back to it at the same time.

The Ride to the Olympics Foundation became our charity anchor. Every Polo Classic ticket, every sponsor partnership, every suit that walks onto that field contributes to their work.

Today, the Polo Classic is Atlanta's annual intersection of fashion, sport, and community. The suits on that field are built in this atelier.
