VSL Preview: Loom Cold-Open Script

For: Miguel Wilson, Miguel Wilson Collection Type: 3-Minute Cold Loom / VSL (Andrei to Miguel) Prepared: May 2026 Status: Confidential

Context

This is a Loom-style cold-open video. Andrei records it and shares it alongside the IG DM and/or email. The screen shows the hub demo page at https://andrei170.github.io/miguel-wilson-collection-hub/ throughout.

Duration target: 2.5 to 3 minutes. No filler.

Script

[SCREEN: Hub demo page loading. Andrei visible in corner webcam.]

"Miguel, my name is Andrei. I built you a Polo Classic and wedding party funnel mockup for Miguel Wilson Collection. This video is 3 minutes. Here is what I found and why I built it."

[PAUSE. Scroll to hero section of hub.]

"First, the credential gap. You have Vogue editorial, Modern Luxury Atlanta, Jezebel. You have dressed Cam Newton, 2 Chainz, and Waka Flocka. You have the Polo Classic, five years running, with the Ride to the Olympics Foundation.

I ran five buyer queries in AI search: 'best couture menswear Atlanta,' 'best custom suit Atlanta,' 'celebrity bespoke menswear designer,' 'wedding suits Atlanta couture,' 'best black-owned luxury menswear.' Zero citations. Tom James and Knot Standard take every answer.

That is not a talent problem. It is a structured data problem. Tom James has LocalBusiness schema for their Atlanta showroom and Service schema for their tailoring process. You have better credentials and no schema. That is fixable in two to three weeks."

[SCROLL to the service lanes section of the hub demo.]

"Second, the Polo Classic funnel. This event draws Atlanta's luxury buyer demographic. There is no email capture connected to it right now. Every attendee is a one-touch impression. What I built here is a dedicated RSVP page with an email opt-in, a pre-event nurture sequence, and a post-event consultation CTA.

An event of this profile with 500 RSVP captures and a 10% post-event consultation conversion rate is worth roughly 50 consultations per year from one landing page."

[SCROLL to wedding party section.]

"Third, the wedding party loop. A groom booking at your level typically brings a 4 to 10-person groomsmen party. The group rate is a higher-margin, lower-acquisition-cost order than a solo consultation. And if those groomsmen get an email 12 months later asking about their first solo suit, 25 to 30% of them book.

There is no structured page or sequence capturing that right now. What I built here is a dedicated wedding party consultation page with clear group pricing language and a 5-email groomsmen follow-up sequence."

[SCROLL to the bottom of the hub, showing the trust strip and press section.]

"The hub you are looking at is a working demo. Real pages, live on GitHub. The schema behind it is ready to deploy on your actual site. The email sequences are written. The Polo Classic RSVP flow is built.

If any of this is interesting, reply to my message or book 20 minutes here: [Calendly or contact link].

I will not follow up more than twice. If the timing is wrong, no problem.

Andrei. AG1 Consulting. https://aiautomatedgrowth.com/#services"

[END. Screen holds on hub demo homepage.]

Delivery Notes

  • Record clean, no background noise. Professional framing.
  • Keep the hub demo loaded in Chrome, not a browser with 30 tabs visible.
  • No virtual background if possible. A clean workspace reads better than a branded blur.
  • If Loom is not available: share as unlisted YouTube or Vimeo. Do not send the file directly.
  • This script runs approximately 2:40 to 3:00 at a moderate speaking pace. Do not rush the credential-gap section; that is where Miguel's ears will perk.
  • The script uses "you" throughout, not "your brand" or "the collection." One-on-one register.

Key Moments (Do Not Cut)

  1. The credential recitation at the start ("Vogue, Modern Luxury, Jezebel, Cam Newton, 2 Chainz, Waka Flocka, Polo Classic") establishes that you have done the work and you are not pitching a generic template.
  2. The "zero citations" line with the explicit query list. This is the bombshell. Say it plainly.
  3. The revenue math on the Polo Classic funnel (50 consultations per year from one page). Specific is credible.
  4. The closing: "I will not follow up more than twice." Respect their time. Sets the tone.
